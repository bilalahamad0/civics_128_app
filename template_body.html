      📋 Official USCIS Naturalization Interview Day Checklist

      Ensure you arrive prepared with all necessary documentation and follow proven interview best practices.

      Essential Documents to Bring:
      USCIS Oral Exam Best Practices:
      1. Answer only what is asked: If the question asks for ONE right from the First Amendment, simply say "Freedom of speech." Do not try to recite all five.
      2. Ask for repetition if unclear: You may politely say: "Officer, could you please repeat the question?" Officers are instructed to rephrase or repeat without penalty.
      3. Arrive 15-30 minutes early: Allow ample time for federal building security checkpoints. Dress in neat, professional business attire.
      4. Remember English components: In addition to civics, you will read 1 out of 3 English sentences correctly and write 1 out of 3 English sentences correctly.
